Downstream probes on OpenWAM representations

Seven OpenWAM architectures differ only in how the action branch is wired to the video branch. We ask whether that wiring changes what the video branch encodes, by probing every layer of every architecture on three downstream tasks, with VideoMAEv2, DINOv2 and D4RT probed identically as yardsticks. Every probe reads the full, unpooled token grid of one layer and is trained on 5 episodes per task.

How to read the scale

Absolute values are low on every task for every model, including the specialist encoders: this protocol uses 5 training episodes per task and coarse token grids (OpenWAM 8 × 10 per frame), so it measures what a tiny probe can read out, not the ceiling of the task. The meaningful scale is therefore the span the three reference encoders occupy. The tiles below express the spread across the 7 OpenWAM architectures as a fraction of that span.

Task 2 · geometry

Depth estimation

Metrics

AbsRel ↓
Mean |predicted − true| / true depth per pixel. 0.05 means 5% error on average. The reference encoders span 0.053–0.079 (Agilex) and 0.091–0.139 (Franka).
δ1 ↑
Share of pixels within a factor 1.25 of the true depth.
Pairwise ↑
DA-2K-style relative depth: of 200 random pixel pairs per frame whose true depths differ by ≥ 10%, the share ordered correctly. 0.5 is chance; every model scores above 0.9, so this metric saturates and separates models least.
Specialist ablation
Two extra rows. Depth Anything 3 encoder: the same linear probe on the tokens of DA3-BASE's ViT-B backbone (blocks 3/5/7/9/11, 12 × 16 tokens at 336 × 448 input), i.e. a backbone trained for depth, probed like everything else. Depth Anything 3 zero-shot: DA3's own predicted depth, no training on RoboTwin at all; DA3 predicts scale-free depth, so each frame is aligned to the GT before AbsRel / δ1: the main row uses a least-squares scale + shift per frame (the MiDaS / Depth Anything protocol for relative depth, applied in depth space), the second row a single median scale factor; pairwise accuracy needs no alignment. It is a ceiling for what a dedicated depth model reads from these frames, not a probe.
Probe
Linear head per token → log depth, bilinearly upsampled to 120 × 160, trained 60 epochs on 5 episodes per task, scored on aligned frames 4, 8, …, 32.

Task 3 · objects

Target-object segmentation

Metric

IoU ↑
Overlap / union between the predicted and true mask of the manipulated object, averaged over frames where it is visible. The object covers only 1–2% of the image and the probe sees an 8 × 10 (OpenWAM) or 14–16² (references) token grid upsampled to 120 × 160, so even DINOv2's best layer reaches 0.16 on Agilex and 0.12 on Franka. Read values against that span, not against 1.0.
Specialist ablation
Two extra rows. SAM ViT-H encoder: the same linear probe on the tokens of SAM's image encoder (hidden states after its 4 global-attention blocks and the 256-d neck embedding its mask decoder consumes; 64 × 64 tokens pooled to 16 × 16). SAM zero-shot: SAM prompted with the GT target's bounding box and centroid (oracle prompts, best of its 3 masks by SAM's own score), no training; it is a ceiling for prompted segmentation of these objects, not a probe, and the probe rows never see the prompt.
Probe
Linear head per token → mask logit, class-balanced BCE, 60 epochs on 5 episodes per task.

Task 4 · motion

3D scene flow

Metrics

EPE3D ↓
3D endpoint error in mm: for a query pixel at probe frame k, the distance between the predicted and true 3D displacement of that point to frame k+1 (16 raw frames ≈ 0.5 s). Ground truth comes from the per-entity poses recorded in the replay (_extra.hdf5), so it is exact for every pixel on a known entity. Queries are stratified into robot links, moving objects (|Δ| > 1 mm) and static scene, 16 each per step.
zero-motion, rel
EPE of predicting "nothing moved", and EPE3D divided by it. rel < 1 means the probe reads real motion out of the tokens; the static group is where zero-motion is already perfect.
Probe
Attention read-out whose query encodes (u, v); it attends over the tokens of frames k and k+1 (frame-tagged) and regresses Δxyz plus a visibility logit. 60 epochs on 5 episodes per task; steps k = 1..7 for every model.

Task 5 · tracking

2D point tracking (TAPVid metrics)

Metrics

AJ ↑
TAPVid Average Jaccard: over pixel thresholds {1, 2, 4, 8, 16}, the Jaccard of "predicted visible and within threshold" against "truly visible", averaged. Penalises wrong positions and wrong visibility together.
δ_avg ↑
Fraction of truly visible queries whose predicted 2D endpoint is within threshold, averaged over the same thresholds (320 × 240 frame).
OA ↑
Occlusion accuracy: how often the probe's visible / not-visible call at k+1 is right.
Probe
The same probe as Task 4; its 3D prediction is projected through the camera for the 2D endpoint and its 4th output is the visibility call.

Caveats

Limits of this evidence

  • One probe seed, one train/test split. Architecture gaps of the size observed cannot be ranked until bootstrap intervals over test episodes are added.
  • Success rates are single evaluations from reports/performance.md; their own uncertainty is unknown.
  • Probe budget. Classification uses 10 epochs (about 5 points short of convergence); depth and segmentation use 60 because their linear heads were far from converged at 10. The same budget applies to every model and layer.
  • Tasks 4–5 use the per-entity poses added to the dataset on 2026-10-05; the probe's own metrics are listed in each section.
