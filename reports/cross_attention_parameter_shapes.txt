Conclusion
==========
The video branch DOES have a learned projection before the action-side
cross-attention: video features are projected from 3072 to 1024.
Then the cross-attention K/V layers project 1024 back to the common attention
width 3072 (= 24 heads x 128 head dimension).
The attention output is projected from 3072 back to the action residual width
1024.
