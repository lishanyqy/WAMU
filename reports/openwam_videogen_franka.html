Summary

92 clips per model, the same clips for every model (RoboTwin Franka clean_50 (another robot: out of distribution); per task 2 random episodes, one random full window each). Each model generates the 9-frame clip on the 384 × 320 canvas (head camera on top, two wrist cameras below) from the clean first frame, the prompt and the proprio state, with 20 denoising steps; frames 1–8 are compared with the ground truth. "Copy first frame" predicts frame 0 for every later frame and is the trivial baseline; the VAE round trip is the reconstruction ceiling. The last column is the model's LPIPS minus the baseline's: negative means better than doing nothing.
