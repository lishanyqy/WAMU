Clean2Rand probes: what survives domain randomization

Eight clean-trained OpenWAM models (pretrained-robot-only, cross-attn-direct and cross-attn-softmax were tested on rand only, so they have no clean score or retention). Each probe is trained once on clean scenes, then tested on held-out clean scenes and on the same tasks replayed with randomized backgrounds, lighting, table height and cluttered tables. All numbers are frozen video-backbone tokens (head camera, unpooled, σ = 0.5), probed layer by layer.

Probe training250 clean episodes (the existing 10% split, 5 per task)
Clean test500 held-out clean episodes, 10 per task (seed 0)
Rand test500 randomized episodes, 10 per task, replayed GT
Probesdepth & segmentation 60 ep · flow/tracking 60 ep · classification 10 ep · all 30 layers

    Notes

    Segmentation target on randomized scenes. The clean rule picks the frame-0 object whose mask centroid moves furthest. With clutter on the table, a pressed bell or clock barely moves and a partly occluded distractor won in 19% of episodes, so distractors (from each episode's official clutter list) are excluded; 497/500 rand targets are task objects afterwards. Clean targets are unchanged.

    Layer choice is optimistic. “Best layer” is picked on the test set itself, as in the clean report. The toggle above shows the stricter reading, where the rand score is taken at the layer that was best on clean.

    Restored rows. 40 rand flow rows (self-attn-detach layers 20–29, pretrained self-attn all layers) were lost to two probe processes writing the same file and were restored from the run log at log precision (0.1 mm, 3 decimals).

    Single seed. One probe seed and one noise draw per episode; differences of about 0.01 in pairwise order or 0.5 mm in EPE are within run-to-run noise seen in the clean report.
