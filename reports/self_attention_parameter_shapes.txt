Video-side interpretation
=========================
The video stream remains in its native 3072 residual width. Its Wan video
self-attention uses Q/K/V and output projections of shape (3072, 3072), i.e.
3072 -> 3072 for Q/K/V and 3072 -> 3072 for the output.

Joint attention data flow
=========================
video tokens:  (B, T_video, 3072) -> Q/K/V in 3072 attention space
action tokens: (B, T_action, 1024) -> Q/K/V in 3072 attention space
tokens are mixed in the shared 24 x 128 attention space; the action branch
is projected back to 1024 by self_attn.o.

Unlike the cross-attention model, there is no action_backbone.video_projs
layer in this checkpoint. Video is not first reduced to 1024.
